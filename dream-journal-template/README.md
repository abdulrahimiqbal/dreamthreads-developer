# Free dream journal template

Download the DreamThreads context-first dream journal template in the format
that fits your practice:

- [`dream-journal-template-fillable.pdf`](dream-journal-template-fillable.pdf)
  — two pages, 20 fillable fields, and a print-friendly layout;
- [`dream-journal-template.docx`](dream-journal-template.docx) — editable in
  Word or Google Docs;
- [`dream-journal-template.md`](dream-journal-template.md) — import into
  Notion or any Markdown notes app;
- [`dream-journal-log.csv`](dream-journal-log.csv) — one dream per row for
  filtering and pattern review.

The canonical guide explains what to record and why:
<https://mydreamthreads.xyz/dream-journal/template>.

Dream reflections are to sit with, not predictions or diagnoses. Do not commit
completed templates or private dream content to a public repository.
